## App protection
Curfy protects only the apps you select, during your configured protection window. It does not lock your whole phone or measure sleep.

If an app is shielded, manually open Curfy from your iPhone Home Screen. Complete the uninterrupted 120-second hold for the app you want to access. Releasing early or leaving the hold interrupts it. A completed hold provides up to 15 minutes of access, ending sooner if the protection window finishes. Other selected apps remain protected.

If protection is not behaving as expected, open Curfy and check your selected apps and protection hours. Check that Screen Time authorization remains enabled. If Curfy displays a recovery message, retry it rather than deleting saved settings. Include the time of the issue when contacting support.

## Premium purchase or restore
Curfy Premium is a one-time purchase for protecting multiple individual apps. The purchase screen displays Apple's localized price.

Use Restore purchases in Curfy Settings while signed into the Apple Account used for the purchase. If Apple reports the purchase as pending, wait for its approval or completion. Do not purchase again solely because ownership has not yet appeared. Contact support if the problem persists.

## Leaderboard and privacy
Leaderboard participation is optional. You can leave from Settings without losing local history, your journal, app protection or Premium. If cloud removal is pending, use Finish leaving leaderboard to retry.

Delete Everything requests removal of your cloud account and local Curfy data. It is different from leaving the leaderboard. If deletion reports an error, keep the error message and contact support if retry does not resolve it. Never send authentication tokens or passwords.

## Contact support
The owner must supply the monitored email before publication. In a support request, include your Curfy version, iPhone model, iOS version, what you expected, what happened, and the approximate time/time zone. A redacted screenshot can help. Do not include private journal text, passwords, payment-card details or access tokens.

No response-time promise is made until the owner establishes a support schedule.
