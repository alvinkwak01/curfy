Opening a protected app

Open Curfy from your iPhone Home Screen, then choose the protected app. Complete its uninterrupted 120-second hold. Releasing early or leaving the hold interrupts it. A completed hold provides up to 15 minutes of access, ending sooner if your protection window finishes. Other selected apps remain protected.

On supported newer iOS versions, the shield also offers an Open Curfy action. Opening Curfy alone does not unlock an app.

If protection needs attention

Check your selected apps, protection hours and Screen Time authorization. If Curfy displays a recovery message, retry it before changing or deleting saved settings. Contact us if the issue continues.

Premium and restoring purchases

Premium is a one-time purchase for protecting multiple apps. Apple’s localized price appears before purchase. Use Restore purchases in Curfy Settings with the Apple Account that made the purchase.

If a purchase is pending, wait for Apple’s approval or completion. Do not purchase again solely because ownership has not appeared.

Leaderboard and deleting data

Joining is optional. Leave from Settings to stop uploads and request removal of your cloud identity and public profile while preserving local history, your journal, protection and Premium. If cloud removal is pending, use Finish leaving leaderboard to retry.

In Settings, choose Delete Account and confirm Delete Everything. This additionally stops Curfy protection and clears local Curfy data after the required removal steps succeed. If deletion reports an error, retry when online. Contact us if it continues.

Contacting support

Include your Curfy version, iPhone model, iOS version, what you expected, what happened, and the approximate time and time zone. A redacted screenshot can help. Never send passwords, authentication tokens, card details or private journal entries.

For privacy requests or an inappropriate leaderboard name, use the same support email. Include only the public profile information needed to identify the issue.
