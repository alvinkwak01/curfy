This policy covers Curfy and its support website. Curfy is preparing for its first App Store release.

Who is responsible

Alvin Kwak, operating Curfy, is responsible for the personal information described here. Contact sleepingcurfy@gmail.com for privacy and support requests.

Information on your iPhone

Curfy stores protection hours, preferences, app-protection history, journal entries and recovery information on your device. Apple represents selected apps using private selection tokens. Curfy uses them on your device to apply and recover protection. It does not send selected app tokens, journal entries or detailed local history to its leaderboard service.

Local data may be included in device or operating-system backups according to your settings. Curfy’s history describes app-protection activity, not measured sleep.

Optional public leaderboard

Protection and Premium do not require joining. Before the first upload, Curfy explains what becomes public and asks for your choice. If you join, Curfy creates a cloud identity and uploads your display name, protection start time and streak information. Updates then happen automatically. The public profile also includes an identifier and update timestamp. Avoid putting private information in your display name.

Supabase hosts this service in the United States, in its US West region. Requests include technical information such as IP addresses that service providers process for operation and security.

Leave from Settings to pause new uploads and request removal of your cloud identity and public profile. If removal cannot be confirmed, Curfy keeps a retry option and does not report completion.

Purchases and notifications

Apple processes purchases and restores. Curfy checks Apple’s entitlement information to determine Premium access; Curfy does not receive your payment-card details. If you grant notification permission, Curfy can schedule local reminders. Manage that permission in iPhone Settings.

Support and this website

Information you send to sleepingcurfy@gmail.com is used to respond and investigate your request. Google provides the support mailbox. Please send only the information needed for your request.

This static website does not include advertising, analytics scripts, contact forms or an app-account login. The website is hosted using Cloudflare Pages. Hosting providers process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser information, to deliver and protect the pages. Curfy does not add advertising or cross-site tracking scripts.

Deletion and retention

Local information remains until you clear it through Curfy or your device’s controls; device backups are managed separately. Your public profile remains until removal succeeds. Settings → Delete Account → Delete Everything requests cloud-account removal, stops Curfy protection and clears local data; failures remain retryable.

We keep support correspondence for the time needed to resolve requests, follow up on recurring issues, and satisfy applicable recordkeeping obligations. Service providers retain operational and security logs and backups according to their service settings and security or legal requirements. Retention depends on the purpose, an unresolved incident or request, and applicable legal obligations. We do not promise that deletion from the live service immediately erases every provider backup. Contact us for information about a specific record or deletion request.

Why information is used and where it goes

We use information to provide the features you request, maintain security and reliability, answer support requests, and meet legal obligations. Where a legal basis is required, optional public leaderboard participation is based on your consent; providing requested services is based on performance of the service, and security and support are based on legitimate interests where permitted. Withdrawing leaderboard consent stops future uploads and does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

Your public profile can be seen and copied by other people. Service providers process information for their respective services, and information may also be disclosed when legally required or necessary to protect rights and safety. Curfy does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

The cloud service is hosted in the United States. Other providers may process information in countries outside your own. Supabase’s Data Processing Addendum describes its transfer safeguards, including applicable standard contractual clauses. Contact us for information about applicable safeguards or your rights.

Your choices and requests

You can decline or leave the leaderboard, manage notification and Screen Time permissions, and request deletion. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to access, correct, export, restrict or object to processing of personal information, and to complain to a relevant privacy authority. Contact the support inbox to make a request. We may need enough information to verify that it concerns your data; do not email authentication secrets.

Policy updates

This policy shows its effective date above. Material changes to data use will be explained through the app or this website, with additional consent requested where required.
